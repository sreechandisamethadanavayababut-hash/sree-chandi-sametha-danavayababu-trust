🛕 Alaya Punar Nirmanam Sankusthapana

🙏 Temple construction Ceremony

🛕 Alaya Punar Nirmanam Sankusthapana is the sacred ritual performed for the construction of an existing temple. This ceremony marks the divine beginning of rebuilding the temple with renewed spiritual energy.

Devotees gathered together and participated in this holy ritual to seek blessings for the successful completion of the temple reconstruction.

🙏 This ritual signifies the rebirth of the temple 🙏
